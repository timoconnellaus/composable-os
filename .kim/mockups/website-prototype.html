Questions

Who owns the software and the data?

The data is yours, and you can have the code for your product whenever you want it. The source of the platform underneath is public.

What if we stop working with Zero BS AI?

You take the code and the data with you. Your team or another developer can carry on with the same toolkit.

Can the AI change things without asking?

Only what your admins allow. By default, new fields, bulk edits, deletes and outgoing messages wait for a person.

What does it cost?

Once it's built, one subscription that covers hosting, updates, fixes, changes and support. The figure depends on what we compose for you, so you get it after the call.

Can it connect to the systems we already use?

Yes: email, calendars, Slack, payments and webhooks today, each through a connection you control.
