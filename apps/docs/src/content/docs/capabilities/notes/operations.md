The core operations that are this capability's (named `note.`, `carer.`, `file.`). Each links to its full reference, with its input and output.

| Operation | What it does | Audiences | Risk | Permission |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [`note.open`](/reference/operations/note/#noteopen) | Open a note to read its text: a sensitive note is listed on timelines without it. Only a person who may read the note (its author, admins, and people the business named one by one) can open it; anyone else gets not found. Every opening is recorded in the audit log, without the text. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`note.kinds.list`](/reference/operations/note/#notekindslist) | The kinds of note the business keeps (Note, Session note, Sensitive, From the client and its own), in the composer's order: each with its template's sections (label, hint, required), the level a note of it starts at and the levels it may have. Retired kinds are left out unless includeRetired. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`note.kinds.save`](/reference/operations/note/#notekindssave) | Write a kind of note: a new one (no id), or a change to one's name, the level a note of it starts at (defaultLevel), the lowest level a note of it may have (lowestLevel) and its template (sections given whole and in order, at most 12: label, an optional hint, required), and whether photos and PDFs may be attached to its notes (filesAllowed). A changed level applies to notes written from now on. Needs notes:manage. An assistant's or MCP client's write always waits for a person who holds it too, and may change only the name and the template: who reads a kind's notes, and what may be attached, is a person's to set. | `staff` | `write` | `notes:manage` |
| [`note.kinds.retire`](/reference/operations/note/#notekindsretire) | Retire a kind of note (retired: true): the composer no longer offers it and its notes keep it. retired: false offers it again. Note, the kind of a note written without one, is never retired. Needs notes:manage; a person only. | `staff` | `destructive` | `notes:manage` |
| [`file.checkUpload`](/reference/operations/file/#filecheckupload) | Whether an upload of a file of this size may go ahead: onto this note (its author or someone allowed to manage notes, a kind that takes files, at most 20 files a note), or waiting for a note not yet saved; and within the business's storage. The upload route asks it before reading the file. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:write` |
| [`file.upload`](/reference/operations/file/#fileupload) | Store a photo or PDF a person uploaded to attach to a note: a JPEG, PNG or WebP picture (at most 10 MB) or a PDF (at most 20 MB), typed from its bytes, never its name; location, camera and other metadata are removed from a picture before it is kept. With a note, it is attached to that note at once (its author or someone allowed to manage notes; a kind that takes files; at most 20 a note). Without one, it waits for the note it goes with (activity.log's files) and is deleted if no note takes it within a day. The file comes from the upload route, not from this input. A person only. | `staff` | `write` | `records:write` |
| [`file.serve`](/reference/operations/file/#fileserve) | Whether the caller may be served one record file now, and how: only a person who may read the note it is attached to, at this moment (its level, a private note's owner), and never a note in the Trash. Anything else is not found. Serves of a file on a note kept from the whole staff are recorded in the audit log. The file route (`/api/files/<id>`) streams it. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`file.delete`](/reference/operations/file/#filedelete) | Remove a file from its note, at once and for good (it does not go to the Trash): its note's author or someone allowed to manage notes, or the person who uploaded it while it waits for its note. A person only. Returns the file's id. | `staff` | `destructive` | `records:write` |
| [`file.storage.get`](/reference/operations/file/#filestorageget) | How much storage the files attached to notes take, how many there are, and the limit past which uploads are refused. People allowed to manage notes. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `notes:manage` |
| [`file.storage.setCap`](/reference/operations/file/#filestoragesetcap) | Change the limit on what the files attached to notes may take, in bytes (1 GB to 10 TB; default 10 GB). Uploads past it are refused; nothing already kept is deleted. A human admin in the app only. | `staff` | `write` | `settings:manage` |
| [`carer.set`](/reference/operations/carer/#carerset) | Say that a staff account looks after a person (their coach): from then on it reads the person's notes kept to their coaches, and stops when the row is ended with carer.end. Choose it on the person's page; it is never worked out from bookings. Refused when the account already looks after them. Only a person on the staff with people:link can do this: never the assistant, an MCP client or code. Audited. | `staff` | `write` | `people:link` |
| [`carer.end`](/reference/operations/carer/#carerend) | End a looks-after row (carer.list gives its id): from the next read the account no longer reads the person's notes kept to their coaches, except the ones it wrote. The row is kept, ended, with who ended it. Only a person on the staff with people:link can do this. Audited. | `staff` | `write` | `people:link` |
| [`carer.list`](/reference/operations/carer/#carerlist) | Who looks after a person (their coaches): the staff accounts that read the person's notes kept to their coaches, with who set each and when, live ones first, and (with ended) the rows that have ended. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
