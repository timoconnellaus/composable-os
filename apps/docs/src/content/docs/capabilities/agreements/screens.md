Its blocks, for client apps (copied with `cos add`) and the admin.

| Block | Skin | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [web-agree](/reference/blocks/web-agree/) | web | Agree to one document (stage 44 step 5), for the web: the version the host gave, its words, a health form's questions, the tick label and its box, the typed name and, for a document agreed to by drawing, the signature pad. |
| [native-agree](/reference/blocks/native-agree/) | native | Agree to one document (stage 44 step 5), for the mobile app: the version the host gave, its words, a health form's questions, the tick label and its box, the typed name and, for a document agreed to by drawing, the signature pad. |
| [web-agreements-due](/reference/blocks/web-agreements-due/) | web | "Before you go on" (stage 44 step 5), for the web: shown after sign-in when the host says something is due (me.agreements.due). |
| [native-agreements-due](/reference/blocks/native-agreements-due/) | native | "Before you go on" (stage 44 step 5), for the mobile app: after sign-in, when the host says something is due (me.agreements.due), each due document in turn, the member's own first, then each child's; "Not now" goes on to the app. |
| [web-my-agreements](/reference/blocks/web-my-agreements/) | web | My agreements (stage 44 step 5), for the web: what the signed-in member, and each child linked to them, has agreed to (each document, the version, when, how and by whom), what is due (with a notice when a document changed and must be agreed to again), the signed PDF, and a health form's answers to update. |
| [native-my-agreements](/reference/blocks/native-my-agreements/) | native | My agreements (stage 44 step 5), for the mobile app: what the signed-in member and each child linked to them has agreed to (the document, the version, when, how and by whom), what is due (with a notice when a document changed), the signed PDF, and a health form's answers to update. |
