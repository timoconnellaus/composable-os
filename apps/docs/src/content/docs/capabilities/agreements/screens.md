Its blocks, for client apps (copied with `cos add`) and the admin, each drawn with dummy data.

### web-agree

Web. Agree to one document (stage 44 step 5), for the web: the version the host gave, its words, a health form's questions, the tick label and its box, the typed name and, for a document agreed to by drawing, the signature pad. [The block](/reference/blocks/web-agree/).

### native-agree

Native. Agree to one document (stage 44 step 5), for the mobile app: the version the host gave, its words, a health form's questions, the tick label and its box, the typed name and, for a document agreed to by drawing, the signature pad. [The block](/reference/blocks/native-agree/).

### web-agreements-due

Web. "Before you go on" (stage 44 step 5), for the web: shown after sign-in when the host says something is due (me.agreements.due). [The block](/reference/blocks/web-agreements-due/).

<img class="cos-screen" src="/screens/web-agreements-due.png" alt="web-agreements-due, drawn with dummy data" width="720" height="731" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

### native-agreements-due

Native. "Before you go on" (stage 44 step 5), for the mobile app: after sign-in, when the host says something is due (me.agreements.due), each due document in turn, the member's own first, then each child's; "Not now" goes on to the app. [The block](/reference/blocks/native-agreements-due/).

<img class="cos-screen cos-screen-native" src="/screens/native-agreements-due.png" alt="native-agreements-due, drawn with dummy data" width="390" height="844" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

### web-my-agreements

Web. My agreements (stage 44 step 5), for the web: what the signed-in member, and each child linked to them, has agreed to (each document, the version, when, how and by whom), what is due (with a notice when a document changed and must be agreed to again), the signed PDF, and a health form's answers to update. [The block](/reference/blocks/web-my-agreements/).

<img class="cos-screen" src="/screens/web-my-agreements.png" alt="web-my-agreements, drawn with dummy data" width="720" height="988" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
