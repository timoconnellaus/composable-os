The core operations that are this capability's (named `agreement.`, `me.agreements.`, `desk.`, `subscription.coolOff`). Each links to its full reference, with its input and output.

| Operation | What it does | Audiences | Risk | Permission |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [`desk.status`](/reference/operations/desk/#deskstatus) | On the desk origin: whether this browser is an enrolled device, and if so its name and use, so the tablet shows Enter code or Enrol. Reads nothing else. Read-only. | `public` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`subscription.coolOff`](/reference/operations/subscription/#subscriptioncooloff) | End a subscription in its cooling-off period, for a full refund of what was paid in it (subscription.coolingOff says whether it may be, and what it owes). Ended at the payment provider now: nothing more is charged, and what it gives stops once the provider confirms. Then each order paid up to the end of the period, and between the ask and the end, is owed back in full, whatever was used; each waits for a person with orders:refund to approve it. The bookings ahead the membership paid for are cancelled as the business's. Refused for a plan with no cooling off, a membership moved over with a continuation offer, a member who has cooled off before, while nothing has been paid (a free trial: cancel instead), and after the period unless one was asked in it. An ask inside the period opens it for 7 days; any end in that time is the cooling off's, and at its deadline it lapses (the ordinary cancel works again). A signed-in customer ends their own (the one they pay for, a child's included) and asks again only inside the period; staff any, with subscriptions:manage, ask again until the deadline, and finish a lapsed one (only what was paid in the period is owed, once the provider confirms the membership ended). Each ask reads the provider first and is a new request to it; nothing is done twice. Refused while a cancel of it waits for a person at the payments gatekeeper. A person decides, never an AI assistant. | `staff`, `customer` | `write` | `subscriptions:manage` |
| [`subscription.coolOffSweep`](/reference/operations/subscription/#subscriptioncooloffsweep) | The host's minute step for cooling offs: one whose call to the payment provider was lost or never made is resolved from the provider's read or asked again (the same ask) until its deadline; at the deadline, or once its latest waiting cancel is rejected or expires with none still waiting, or once its holder has used theirs on another, an open one lapses (every waiting cancel rejected, admins told once); staff's finish of a lapsed one is asked again, waited for, or marked stuck with admins told; then the summary emails. Bounded; nothing when nothing is due. The host's own cron only. | `staff` | `write` | `subscriptions:manage` |
| [`subscription.coolOffRelease`](/reference/operations/subscription/#subscriptioncooloffrelease) | The host's follow-up for a membership ended in its cooling-off period: cancels the next bookings ahead it paid for, as the business's, until none is left. The host's own cron only. | `staff` | `write` | `subscriptions:manage` |
| [`agreement.list`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementlist) | List the business's agreement documents (terms, waivers, health forms, a parent's consent): each with its kind, state (draft, live or retired), the version in effect now and any scheduled one, and the agreement points it is attached to. Titles and the business's own text only: nothing here names a member. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`agreement.get`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementget) | One agreement document: its summary, the working copy (the draft the editor changes, with its hash) and its versions, newest first, without their bodies (agreement.version.get reads one). Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`agreement.version.get`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementversionget) | One published version of an agreement document, exactly as people are shown it: title, body, tick label, questions, how a person agrees, and when it took or takes effect. A version never changes. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`agreement.points`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementpoints) | The agreement points installed packs declare (buying a membership, making a booking): the places a business may attach its documents, each with its label, the kinds of document it suggests and the objects that declare it. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`agreement.create`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementcreate) | Start a new agreement document as a draft: its title and kind (terms, waiver, health for a health questions form, guardian for a parent's consent, other). The kind never changes. Its working copy is then written with agreement.draft.save; nothing is asked of anyone until a person publishes it and attaches it to an agreement point. The platform ships no wording: the business writes its own. | `staff` | `write` | `agreements:manage` |
| [`agreement.draft.save`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementdraftsave) | Write an agreement document's working copy: any of its title, body (rich text), tick label (the words beside the box), how a person agrees (typed name, or a drawn signature), who agrees (the participant or the payer), for whom (everyone, or only a dependant), and for a health form its questions and after how many months it is asked again. Only what is given changes. It changes no published version and nothing anyone is asked: a person publishes. draftRev, when given, is the copy's rev the change was made against: a copy saved by someone else since is refused. Two saves of the same rev never both apply. The assistant may write it for someone who holds agreements:manage. | `staff` | `write` | `agreements:manage` |
| [`agreement.publishPreview`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementpublishpreview) | What publishing an agreement document's working copy would make: the exact snapshot (every field a version holds), its number, whether it is the first, which fields changed against the newest version, the hash and rev to pass to agreement.publish, and why it cannot be published yet if it cannot. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`agreement.publish`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementpublish) | Publish an agreement document's working copy as its next version, which nobody can change afterwards. change: small (earlier acceptances still count) or agree_again (everyone must agree to this version before their next booking or purchase); a first version is first. effectiveFrom (left out: now) schedules it; never in the past or before the newest version's, and never withdrawn. draftHash and draftRev are the preview's: a copy changed since in any field is refused. A person on the staff with agreements:manage only. Audited. | `staff` | `write` | `agreements:manage` |
| [`agreement.attach`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementattach) | Attach a published agreement document to agreement points (agreement.points lists them), so it is needed there: from step 3 of stage 44, nobody buys or books there without a current acceptance of it. Nothing already bought or booked is touched. Only a person on the staff with agreements:manage can do this. Audited. | `staff` | `write` | `agreements:manage` |
| [`agreement.detach`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementdetach) | Detach an agreement document from agreement points: it is no longer needed there. Acceptances people made are kept. Only a person on the staff with agreements:manage can do this. Audited. | `staff` | `write` | `agreements:manage` |
| [`agreement.retire`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementretire) | Retire an agreement document: it is detached everywhere and needed nowhere, its working copy is frozen, and it cannot be published again (write a new document instead). Its versions and the acceptances people made are kept for good. Only a person on the staff with agreements:manage can do this. Audited. | `staff` | `destructive` | `agreements:manage` |
| [`agreement.takeEffect`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementtakeeffect) | Platform use only: make current the scheduled agreement versions whose time has come (the minute cron), as a person published them. No input names a document or a version. | `staff` | `write` | `agreements:manage` |
| [`me.agreements.due`](/reference/operations/me/#meagreementsdue) | What you, as a signed-in customer, and each child linked to you still have to agree to: each due document's version in effect, to read and agree to with me.agreements.accept. canAgree is false for someone a parent or guardian agrees for. Read-only. | `customer` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`me.agreements.list`](/reference/operations/me/#meagreementslist) | Your agreements, as a signed-in customer, and those of each child linked to you: every acceptance, newest first, with the document, the version, who agreed and how, and whether it is the one that counts now. me.agreements.get reads one. Read-only. | `customer` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`me.agreements.get`](/reference/operations/me/#meagreementsget) | One of your acceptances, or a linked child's, as a signed-in customer: the version as you were shown it, a health form's answers, and the name typed and the signature drawn when you are the one who agreed (another guardian's are not shown). Read-only. | `customer` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`me.agreements.accept`](/reference/operations/me/#meagreementsaccept) | Agree to a version of a document, as a signed-in customer: for yourself, or with forPersonId for a child linked to you. Give the name you type, a signature for a document agreed to by drawing, and every answer for a health form. Recorded for good and never changed. Refused for yourself when a parent or guardian agrees for you. | `customer` | `write` | `records:write` |
| [`agreement.guestVersions`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementguestversions) | The words a visitor buying without an account is asked to agree to: each version named (by the versionId of an agreements_due refusal from a buying call), as it will be recorded: its title, body, tick label, health questions and how it is agreed to. Only the version in effect of a live document attached somewhere and asked of everyone; any other id (an older version, one scheduled for later) is left out. The business's text, never anyone's personal data. Read-only. | `customer`, `public` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`agreement.person`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementperson) | A Person's agreements, as the Person page shows them: each document attached anywhere, current or due (and why: never agreed, agree again, asked again, a guardian must agree), every acceptance (who agreed, how, when; paper marked), and whether they are a minor. Titles and dates only: never a name typed, a signature or health answers. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`agreement.acceptances`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementacceptances) | The acceptances people made, newest first: by document, version, who agreed (themselves, a guardian, paper) and a date range, each with the Person and how and when. Never a name typed, a signature or health answers. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`agreement.due.list`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementduelist) | Who still has to agree to which document: People with a sign-in, a subscription, a family link or the minor mark who have something attached due, those with a live subscription first. Titles and dates only. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`agreement.healthNotes`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementhealthnotes) | Which of these People answered yes on their current health form, as a yes or no each (a mark, nothing of the form). A person on the staff with agreements:health only; every read is audited. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `agreements:health` |
| [`agreement.listHealthNotes`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementlisthealthnotes) | For a list a pack declares (healthNotes on its object: a class's bookings): the host reads the People on the records of that list's own record (value, the class) and says which answered yes on their current health form (a mark only). Pack code inside a tool a person called themselves only; every call is audited. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`agreement.healthReviews`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementhealthreviews) | The People whose current health form has a yes, newest first, for staff with agreements:health to look at (where a health notice sends them). Every read is audited. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `agreements:health` |
| [`agreement.acceptance.get`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementacceptanceget) | One acceptance in full, for a person on the staff: the version shown, the name typed (beside the Person's own name), the signature drawn, and for paper who recorded it. Health answers are agreement.answers.get's. Never an AI's read. Who signed a paper form and its note are shown only with agreements:manage, as is an acceptance kept after an erase. Every read is audited (which acceptance, never what it says). Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`agreement.answers.get`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementanswersget) | A health form's answers, for a person on the staff with agreements:health: each question, yes or no, and a note. Health information: every read is audited (who read whose), and no AI ever reads them. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `agreements:health` |
| [`agreement.recordPaper`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementrecordpaper) | Record that a Person signed a version of a document on paper (printed from that version): the name on the form, the date written on it, who signed it for a child (signedBy), a note, and for a health form the answers typed in. Shown everywhere as recorded from paper by you, never as their own acceptance. A person on the staff with agreements:paper only. | `staff` | `write` | `agreements:paper` |
| [`agreement.setMinor`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementsetminor) | Mark a Person as a minor, or clear the mark: a parent or guardian agrees for them (or staff record paper), never they themselves, and every document attached anywhere is due as for a child. The host holds no date of birth: clear it when they come of age. A person on the staff with people:link only. Audited. | `staff` | `write` | `people:link` |
| [`me.agreements.pdf`](/reference/operations/me/#meagreementspdf) | The signed PDF of one of your acceptances, as a signed-in customer, or a linked child's: the version as you were shown it and the record of who agreed, how and when, with a health form's answers. The name typed and the signature drawn are in it when you are the one who agreed. Built when asked, never stored. Read-only. | `customer` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`agreement.acceptance.pdf`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementacceptancepdf) | The signed PDF of one acceptance, for a person on the staff: the version shown and the record of who agreed, how and when, the name typed and the signature drawn (paper marked as recorded from paper), and a health form's answers only for staff with agreements:health. Every download is audited. Never an AI's read. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`agreement.version.pdf`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementversionpdf) | A version's blank form as a PDF, to print and sign on paper (then agreement.recordPaper): its words, the tick label, a health form's questions with boxes, lines for the name, signature and date, and the version's number and hash at the foot of every page. A person on the staff. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`agreement.copy.preview`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementcopypreview) | Before Email a copy of a paper form: the address the host would send it to (the Person's sign-in address, or their primary email), or, for a child's form, the parents and guardians linked to the child now that you may see and that have an address, each with the address it would go to, to choose one; or why it would not be sent (a child with no such guardian, or no address), and whether a copy was already asked for. Show the address to the person, who confirms it with agreement.copy.send. A person on the staff with agreements:paper. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `agreements:paper` |
| [`agreement.copy.send`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementcopysend) | Email a paper form's copy (Email a copy): the signed PDF goes, as the host's own mail, to the address the host holds for the Person it is for, which agreement.copy.preview showed; for a child's form, to the linked parent or guardian chosen (`guardianId`, one the preview listed), at the address the host holds for them. `confirmTo` is that address as the person saw it: if the host's address changed since (an edit, by anyone), it is refused and shown again. Once only: asking again answers how the first one went. Refused for a child's form with no linked guardian who has an address, for anyone who is not a live guardian of the child, and for a Person with no address. A person on the staff with agreements:paper only. | `staff` | `write` | `agreements:paper` |
| [`agreement.desk.find`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementdeskfind) | Find the Person a desk signing session is for: by their full name exactly (as a name is compared for duplicates: case, accents and middle initials folded) or by one of their email addresses exactly. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `agreements:desk` |

| [`agreement.desk.devices`](/reference/operations/agreement/#agreementdeskdevices) | The devices an admin enrolled for desk signing, to choose the tablet a session is signed on: name and when last seen. agreements:desk. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `agreements:desk` |
