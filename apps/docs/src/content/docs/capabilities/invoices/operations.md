The core operations that are this capability's (named `invoice.`, `quote.`, `creditNote.`). Each links to its full reference, with its input and output.

| Operation | What it does | Audiences | Risk | Permission |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [`quote.create`](/reference/operations/quote/#quotecreate) | Start a quote as a draft: whom it is for (a Person, or an Organisation with its contact Person), an optional site address, the record it is about, a title, notes for the customer, valid-until, a deposit, and lines. A line is a record for sale (a pass type, a ticket type, a price-list item), priced by the host, or a typed line with a description, quantity, unit and unit price; each has a tax rate from the invoice settings and an optional discount. The host works out each line's tax and the totals. A draft has no number and changes freely: nothing is sent until a person sends it. Amounts are integers in the currency's minor unit. | `staff` | `write` | `invoices:write` |
| [`quote.update`](/reference/operations/quote/#quoteupdate) | Change a draft quote: whom it is for, the site address, what it is about, title, notes, valid-until, the deposit, or whether its amounts are typed with or without tax (its lines are priced again). Only what is given changes; null clears. A sent quote is never changed: it is withdrawn and a new one sent. | `staff` | `write` | `invoices:write` |
| [`quote.addLine`](/reference/operations/quote/#quoteaddline) | Add a line to a draft quote: a record for sale (item: its object and id), priced by the host from the record, or a typed line (description, quantity, unit, unitPrice in minor units, as the quote's amounts are typed). Each line has a tax rate (a key from the invoice settings; left out, the default; null, no tax) and an optional discount (an amount or a percentage, with why), taken off before the tax. The host works out the line's tax, rounded half up, and the quote's totals by rate. At most 50 lines. | `staff` | `write` | `invoices:write` |
| [`quote.updateLine`](/reference/operations/quote/#quoteupdateline) | Change a line of a draft quote: its description, quantity, unit, unit price (a typed line's only: a record's line keeps the host's price), tax rate, discount (null removes it) or position (the others move along). Only what is given changes. The host works out the line's tax and the quote's totals again. | `staff` | `write` | `invoices:write` |
| [`quote.removeLine`](/reference/operations/quote/#quoteremoveline) | Remove a line from a draft quote. The others close up, and the totals are worked out again. | `staff` | `write` | `invoices:write` |
| [`quote.delete`](/reference/operations/quote/#quotedelete) | Delete a draft quote and its lines, for good. Only a draft: a quote that has a number is withdrawn instead. | `staff` | `write` | `invoices:write` |
| [`quote.get`](/reference/operations/quote/#quoteget) | One quote with its lines, totals by rate, whom it bills and (once sent) its number and the bill-to as printed. Amounts are integers in the currency's minor unit. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`quote.list`](/reference/operations/quote/#quotelist) | A page of quotes, newest first unless sorted otherwise, without their lines (quote.get has them). Filter and sort on state, number, personId, organisationId, total, createdAt, validUntil. Pass nextCursor back as cursor for the next page. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`invoice.create`](/reference/operations/invoice/#invoicecreate) | Start an invoice as a draft: whom it is for (a Person, or an Organisation with its contact Person), an optional site address, the record it is about, a title, notes for the customer, payment terms (the settings' by default) or a due date, and lines. A line is a record for sale (a pass type, a ticket type, a price-list item), priced by the host, or a typed line with a description, quantity, unit and unit price; each has a tax rate from the invoice settings and an optional discount. The host works out each line's tax and the totals. A draft has no number and changes freely: nothing is owed until a person issues it. Amounts are integers in the currency's minor unit. | `staff` | `write` | `invoices:write` |
| [`invoice.update`](/reference/operations/invoice/#invoiceupdate) | Change a draft invoice: whom it is for, the site address, what it is about, title, notes, payment terms, the due date, or whether its amounts are typed with or without tax (its lines are priced again). Only what is given changes; null clears. An issued invoice is never changed: it is credited. | `staff` | `write` | `invoices:write` |
| [`invoice.addLine`](/reference/operations/invoice/#invoiceaddline) | Add a line to a draft invoice: a record for sale (item: its object and id), priced by the host from the record, or a typed line (description, quantity, unit, unitPrice in minor units, as the invoice's amounts are typed). Each line has a tax rate (a key from the invoice settings; left out, the default; null, no tax) and an optional discount (an amount or a percentage, with why), taken off before the tax. The host works out the line's tax, rounded half up, and the invoice's totals by rate. At most 50 lines. | `staff` | `write` | `invoices:write` |
| [`invoice.updateLine`](/reference/operations/invoice/#invoiceupdateline) | Change a line of a draft invoice: its description, quantity, unit, unit price (a typed line's only: a record's line keeps the host's price), tax rate, discount (null removes it) or position (the others move along). Only what is given changes. The host works out the line's tax and the invoice's totals again. | `staff` | `write` | `invoices:write` |
| [`invoice.removeLine`](/reference/operations/invoice/#invoiceremoveline) | Remove a line from a draft invoice. The others close up, and the totals are worked out again. | `staff` | `write` | `invoices:write` |
| [`invoice.delete`](/reference/operations/invoice/#invoicedelete) | Delete a draft invoice and its lines, for good. Only a draft: a invoice that has a number is credited or voided instead. | `staff` | `write` | `invoices:write` |
| [`invoice.get`](/reference/operations/invoice/#invoiceget) | One invoice with its lines, totals by rate, whom it bills and (once issued) its number and the bill-to as printed. Amounts are integers in the currency's minor unit. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`invoice.list`](/reference/operations/invoice/#invoicelist) | A page of invoices, newest first unless sorted otherwise, without their lines (invoice.get has them). Filter and sort on state, number, personId, organisationId, total, createdAt, kind, issuedOn, dueOn. Pass nextCursor back as cursor for the next page. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`invoice.settings.get`](/reference/operations/invoice/#invoicesettingsget) | The invoice settings: the business's details and bank details as printed on documents, its tax rates (key, label, rate in hundredths of a percent, retired) and the default, whether amounts are typed with or without tax, payment terms, quote validity, deposit invoices' terms, the reminder schedule, the threshold over which a recorded payment tells the admins, the PDF footer, how long documents are kept, and each numbering series (prefix, next number, version). Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`invoice.settings.update`](/reference/operations/invoice/#invoicesettingsupdate) | Change the invoice settings: the business's details and bank details printed on documents, its tax rates (a rate never changes once saved: retire it and add a new key; a rate on any document is retired, never removed), the default rate, whether amounts are typed with or without tax, payment terms, quote validity, deposit invoices' terms, the reminder schedule (or reminders off), the threshold over which a recorded payment tells the admins, the PDF footer, how long documents are kept, and a numbering series' prefix and next number (only ever raised, naming the series' version). Only what is given changes. Documents already sent or issued keep what they were issued with. A change to the bank details is told to every admin. Admins only, and only a person: AI assistants, MCP clients, automations and extensions are refused. | `staff` | `write` | `settings:manage` |
| [`invoice.events`](/reference/operations/invoice/#invoiceevents) | The document event log, oldest first after a cursor: one row per quote sent, invoice or credit note issued, payment applied or reversed (with its payment row and method), credit, refund confirmed, payment paid back and void, each with its amount and when. Rows are never changed or removed and seq only grows: pass the last seq you read as after. For the books and finance reports. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `invoices:payments` |
| [`invoice.issue`](/reference/operations/invoice/#invoiceissue) | Issue a draft invoice: it takes the next number of the invoice series (INV-0042), its bill-to as printed, the business's details and bank details as they are now, its issue date and its due date (the draft's, or today plus its payment terms), and starts its order in the same step: the invoice's lines as the host priced them, its line discounts, and the tax worked out on it. From then on it is never changed: it is credited. Its order does not expire: it is due on the invoice's due date and shows among the overdue after it; places it holds are held until it is paid, credited or voided. A record's price that changed since its line was added, goods, and an agreement the bill-to still needs are refused with why. A line discount needs the discount permission, and over the limit an admin set it waits for approval. An AI assistant can ask; a person approves. Sending it is a separate step. Returns the invoice. | `staff` | `write` | `invoices:send` |
| [`invoice.allowPartPayments`](/reference/operations/invoice/#invoiceallowpartpayments) | Allow (or stop) part payments online on an issued invoice. Without, the payment page asks for the whole balance; with, the payer may pay part of it, at least the minimum set for orders. A payment recorded by staff can be any amount up to the balance either way. Returns the invoice. | `staff` | `write` | `invoices:send` |
| [`invoice.recordPayment`](/reference/operations/invoice/#invoicerecordpayment) | Record a payment of an issued invoice taken another way than the payment page: a bank transfer, cash, a cheque, a card machine. Any amount up to the balance, never more. The invoice becomes part paid or paid, as its order does, and what it bought is given. The payment names you as the person who received it; it is in the invoice's log, on the timeline, and admins are told of one over the amount set in the invoice settings. A person only, with the permission to record invoice payments: AI assistants, MCP clients, automations and extensions are refused. Returns the payment's id and the invoice. | `staff` | `write` | `invoices:payments` |
| [`invoice.reversePayment`](/reference/operations/invoice/#invoicereversepayment) | Reverse a payment recorded on an invoice in error (invoice.recordPayment): a second entry takes it back off, nothing is deleted, and a paid invoice is part paid again with the amount owed. What it gave stays. Not for a payment made on the payment page (credit the invoice instead), nor one a credit note gives money back on. Admins are told. A person only, with the permission to record invoice payments. Returns the reversal's id and the invoice. | `staff` | `write` | `invoices:payments` |
| [`creditNote.create`](/reference/operations/creditnote/#creditnotecreate) | Draft a credit note against an issued invoice: some of its lines (each with a quantity, or all that is left of it), or an amount at one of its tax rates (in minor units, as the invoice's amounts are typed). Its tax is worked out as the invoice's was. Never more than is left to credit of a line, a rate or the invoice. Say why, and how it settles: reduce what is still owed, give back what was paid, or both. A draft changes nothing until a person with the permission to credit issues it. Returns the draft. | `staff` | `write` | `invoices:write` |
| [`creditNote.get`](/reference/operations/creditnote/#creditnoteget) | One credit note with its lines, totals, how it settles and what it gives back per payment. Amounts are integers in the currency's minor unit. Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`creditNote.list`](/reference/operations/creditnote/#creditnotelist) | An invoice's credit notes, drafts and issued, oldest first, without their lines (creditNote.get has them). Read-only. | `staff` | `read` | `records:read` |
| [`creditNote.delete`](/reference/operations/creditnote/#creditnotedelete) | Delete a draft credit note, for good. An issued one is never deleted. | `staff` | `write` | `invoices:write` |
| [`creditNote.issue`](/reference/operations/creditnote/#creditnoteissue) | Issue a draft credit note: it takes the next number of the credit note series and, in the same step, takes off what is still owed on its invoice and gives back the rest from what was paid, as it says. Money paid on the payment page goes back through the Approvals inbox, for a person with the refund permission; money taken another way is owed back until a person pays it back and records it (order.recordRefund). Never more than was paid. An invoice credited in full with nothing paid is credited, its order cancelled and its places given back. A person only, with the permission to credit invoices: AI assistants are refused. Returns the credit note, its invoice and the refunds waiting. | `staff` | `write` | `invoices:credit` |
| [`creditNote.askRefundAgain`](/reference/operations/creditnote/#creditnoteaskrefundagain) | Ask again for what an issued credit note gives back on the payment page, where its refund was rejected, its approval expired or it failed: the same amount, on the same payment, waiting in the Approvals inbox for a person with the refund permission as before. Until then the credit note shows it as owed back. A person only, with the refund permission. Returns the credit note and the refunds waiting. | `staff` | `write` | `orders:refund` |
| [`invoice.void`](/reference/operations/invoice/#invoicevoid) | Void an issued invoice nothing has been paid on: a credit note for everything still owed on it, issued at once; the invoice is void, its order cancelled and its places given back. An invoice with something paid is credited instead (creditNote.create). A person only, with the permission to credit invoices: AI assistants are refused. Returns the credit note and the invoice. | `staff` | `write` | `invoices:credit` |
