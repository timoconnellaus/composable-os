The core operations that are this capability's (named `outside.`). Each links to its full reference, with its input and output.

| Operation | What it does | Audiences | Risk | Permission |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [`outside.settings.get`](/reference/operations/outside/#outsidesettingsget) | Read the booking-system settings: whether the copy is on, which outside membership and pass types make a Person a member (by default every membership, no pass), the business's links (where a member manages their membership, where to book a class) and the largest age a members list counts for; and the membership types the current members list holds, each marked whether it counts. Needs bookingSystem:manage. | `staff` | `read` | `bookingSystem:manage` |
| [`outside.settings.update`](/reference/operations/outside/#outsidesettingsupdate) | Change the booking-system settings: on or off, the outside types that make a Person a member (null for the default: every membership type, no pass), the two links (each an https address, or null), and the largest age a members list counts for (1 to 90 days). Leave a setting out to keep it. A human admin in the app only. | `staff` | `write` | `settings:manage` |
| [`outside.status`](/reference/operations/outside/#outsidestatus) | The state of the booking system's copy: the current members and timetable lists (each with the date its file was exported, its age, when and by whom it was loaded, rows read, refused and kept, and for a members list whether it is too old to count), how many outside people are kept and linked, and how many people and coaches wait in To match. With no list loaded every count is 0. Counts and dates only, no names. Needs bookingSystem:manage. | `staff` | `read` | `bookingSystem:manage` |
| [`outside.matches`](/reference/operations/outside/#outsidematches) | To match: the outside people and coaches the host could not link by address, each with why (several_people: more than one Person or staff account has the address; shared_address: several outside people share it, as a family on one mailbox; no_address: the list gives none) and the candidates (Persons, or staff accounts) to choose from. Nobody is linked until a person links them with outside.link. Paged by ref. Needs bookingSystem:manage. | `staff` | `read` | `bookingSystem:manage` |
| [`outside.link`](/reference/operations/outside/#outsidelink) | Link an outside person from the members list to a Person, or a coach from the timetable list to a staff account, by hand, with why. A link made by a person is kept across loads by ref, whatever the address. It changes nothing on the Person: no name, address or phone, no customer account, nothing sent. Removes them from To match. A person with bookingSystem:manage only, never an AI. | `staff` | `write` | `bookingSystem:manage` |
| [`outside.unlink`](/reference/operations/outside/#outsideunlink) | Unlink an outside person or coach, with why: they stay in the copy linked to nobody while they count (a member, a live pass, booked), and the host never links them by address again (the decision is kept by ref across loads) until a person links them with outside.link. Removes them from To match. A person with bookingSystem:manage only, never an AI. | `staff` | `write` | `bookingSystem:manage` |
| [`outside.lists.remove`](/reference/operations/outside/#outsidelistsremove) | Remove the booking system's copy: the current members list (its outside people, their memberships and passes, membership types, links and To match), the timetable list (classes, class types, bookings, coaches and their To match), or all of it, which is what disconnecting the booking system means for a loaded list. The list stops counting at once and its rows are deleted at once, 2000 a statement and at most 200000 a call: when done is false, the daily maintenance finishes the rest, or call again with finish: true (nothing reads it meanwhile, and a list loaded since is never touched). Kept: the settings, the lists' log (counts only, 30 days), erased people's suppressions (so no later list brings them back), staff's unlink decisions (a hash, no person) and the timeline. Nothing is written to the booking system. A person with bookingSystem:manage only, never an AI, an MCP client, an extension or an automation. | `staff` | `write` | `bookingSystem:manage` |
| [`outside.suppression.lift`](/reference/operations/outside/#outsidesuppressionlift) | Lift the suppression of an outside ref an erase left: when the booking system has given an erased person's old ref to someone new, every load drops that ref (counted as previously erased) until a person lifts it. The new person is then kept and matched as anyone is. The erased person's addresses stay suppressed. This undoes an erase's protection for that ref: if the booking system still lists the erased person under it, they come back. Needs both bookingSystem:manage and records:erase (the erase's own permission), a person only, never an AI, an MCP client, an extension or an automation. | `staff` | `write` | `bookingSystem:manage` |
| [`outside.file.preview`](/reference/operations/outside/#outsidefilepreview) | The dry run of a list exported from the booking system: decodes the file by its content, maps its columns (the given mapping, else the saved one, else a suggestion from the headers), and says what the load would do against the current list (new, changed, unchanged, removed; not kept; dropped because the person was erased; who would link by address and who would wait in To match) and every refused row with why. Writes nothing and keeps nothing of the file. Pass its fingerprint, with the same file, mapping and export date, to outside.file.load. A person with bookingSystem:manage only, never an AI. | `staff` | `read` | `bookingSystem:manage` |
| [`outside.file.load`](/reference/operations/outside/#outsidefileload) | Load a list exported from the booking system, as its dry run showed: the same file, mapping and export date, and the dry run's fingerprint. The host plans again and refuses, writing nothing, unless the plan is the one the dry run showed (the file, the copy, an erase or a Person's address changed since). The list replaces the current one of its kind in one batch: rows not in the new file go, with their links and matches; nothing of an erased person is kept. The mapping is saved for the next export of the same shape. The file itself is not kept; the audit records counts only. A person with bookingSystem:manage only, never an AI, an MCP client, Code Mode, an extension or an automation. | `staff` | `write` | `bookingSystem:manage` |
