Screens a shipped pack declares are built into the admin and shown to whoever could call every tool in `opens`. Blocks are the pack's screens for client apps, copied with `cos add`.

## Staff screens

This pack declares no staff screens.

## Blocks

Each drawn with dummy data.

### ticketing-checkout

Web. Choosing tickets and paying, for a site (stage 27): quantities per ticket type, pay in full or by deposit when every chosen type offers one, then the payment provider's page. [The block](/reference/blocks/ticketing-checkout/).
