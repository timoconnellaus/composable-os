Routine mail is email about this pack's records that the host sends without a person's approval; an admin can turn each kind off.

## Routine message kinds

| Kind | Label | About | When and to whom | Values it fills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `receipt` | Receipts | `submission` | When someone sends answers to a form whose receipt is on, a short email to the address they gave (or signed in with) saying the answers arrived, naming the business and the form and nothing they typed. At most three a day to one address; never for an anonymous form, answers kept as spam, or answers staff entered. | `business`, `form` |
