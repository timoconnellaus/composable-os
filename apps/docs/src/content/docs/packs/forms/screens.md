Screens a shipped pack declares are built into the admin and shown to whoever could call every tool in `opens`. Blocks are the pack's screens for client apps, copied with `cos add`.

## Staff screens

This pack declares no staff screens.

## Blocks

Each drawn with dummy data.

### forms-form

Web. A form to fill in, for the business's own website (stage 29 step 4): any published form, with every question kind the question schema has, then its thank-you. [The block](/reference/blocks/forms-form/).

### forms-native-form
