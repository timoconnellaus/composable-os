Settings are typed, edited in Settings → Extensions. A setting that is a person's to change waits for a person when anyone else changes it.

| Setting | Type | Label | Default | Limits | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `coolingOffDays` | `number` | Cooling-off period (days) | `0` | min 0; max 30; a person's to change | A new member may end their membership within this many days of joining, counted to the end of the last day, for a full refund of what they paid; each refund is approved by a person who may refund. Not for passes, or for members moving over with a continuation offer; once per member. 0: none. Say it in your membership terms; whether a law asks for one, and how long, is for your lawyer. Shortening it never shortens the period of someone who joined before. |
