Stages that have a spec and no pack or capability pages on this site yet, from [docs/roadmap.md](https://github.com/timoconnellaus/composable-os/blob/main/docs/roadmap.md). Pieces of them that are already in the code appear in the reference (operations, behaviours, blocks).

- **Shared inbox, Messaging and Help desk** (stage 36): One inbox for email, SMS and in-app chat; campaigns; support requests with replies and response targets. [Its spec](https://github.com/timoconnellaus/composable-os/blob/main/docs/stages/36-inbox.md).
- **Outside practitioners** (stage 43): Practitioners the business invites (a physiotherapist, a dietitian) as a fourth kind of caller with their own sign-in; a client's consent (a care link) before anything is seen, ended at once; each practitioner sees only their own work and whether the client did it; coaches see restrictions and the exercise list, never notes; rehab and habits as Programs' programs, logged like training; restrictions, nutrition plans and private notes in a Care pack; sharing between practitioners only by the client; one person as practitioner, coach and member kept apart; the Oceania location hint for new installs. [Its spec](https://github.com/timoconnellaus/composable-os/blob/main/docs/stages/43-practitioners.md).
- **My account** (stage 48): The customer's own account area on the business's website and in its app: one shell copied with `cos add web-account`, with "Needs your attention" first (eight kinds worked out by the host: a document to agree to, a failed card, a payment to confirm, a deposit's balance, an unfinished payment, a pass expiring within 7 days or used up, a trial ending), then the sections of every installed pack (declared in its manifest, shown by the host only to a customer who has records there) and base sections: details, sign-in and devices, family, orders (the one list of a customer's orders), payment method and agreements; changing the sign-in address by a code to the new address after a recent sign-in, the old address told, staff able to start it; deleting the account with a preview, a membership ending at the period already paid for and nothing refunded; money in the app opens the website; a parent sees and books for a child; receipts wait for stage 35. [Its spec](https://github.com/timoconnellaus/composable-os/blob/main/docs/stages/48-my-account.md).
- **Appointments** (stage 49): One-to-one sessions with a coach or an outside practitioner: services, open hours, free times, online booking, late-cancel rule; the `occupies` claim on a room. [Its spec](https://github.com/timoconnellaus/composable-os/blob/main/docs/stages/49-appointments.md).
- **Till** (stage 50): Selling at the desk: desk orders over the Shop catalogue, a card reader, cash with a float and a count, member tabs. [Its spec](https://github.com/timoconnellaus/composable-os/blob/main/docs/stages/50-till.md).
- **Kiosk check-in** (stage 51): Members check themselves in on a shared tablet; the enrolled shared device that the till and desk signing also use. [Its spec](https://github.com/timoconnellaus/composable-os/blob/main/docs/stages/51-kiosk-check-in.md).
- **Journeys** (stage 53): Automatic sequences of messages and tasks (welcome, not seen, win-back), approved by a person once. [Its spec](https://github.com/timoconnellaus/composable-os/blob/main/docs/stages/53-journeys.md).
- **Reviews** (stage 54): Asking for reviews after a number of classes; private feedback; published reviews on the site. [Its spec](https://github.com/timoconnellaus/composable-os/blob/main/docs/stages/54-reviews.md).
- **Website builder** (stage 55): A business edits its own pages, menus and colours from the admin; a hosted site for businesses with no code of their own. [Its spec](https://github.com/timoconnellaus/composable-os/blob/main/docs/stages/55-website-builder.md).
- **Events and venue hire** (stage 57): Hiring out a space without double booking; run sheets and crew shifts for the business's own events. [Its spec](https://github.com/timoconnellaus/composable-os/blob/main/docs/stages/57-events-venue-hire.md).
- **Timesheets and leave** (stage 58): One time-entry record for hours worked at the business or on a job; approval; leave types and balances; pay from approved recorded hours. [Its spec](https://github.com/timoconnellaus/composable-os/blob/main/docs/stages/58-timesheets-leave.md).
- **Accounting sync** (stage 61): Daily summaries, payouts and invoices sent to an accounting system through a gatekeeper; a person approves at first. [Its spec](https://github.com/timoconnellaus/composable-os/blob/main/docs/stages/61-accounting-sync.md).
- **App publishing** (stage 63): A client's iPhone and Android apps in the stores under the client's own accounts, and keeping them updated. [Its spec](https://github.com/timoconnellaus/composable-os/blob/main/docs/stages/63-app-publishing.md).
- **Screen versions** (stage 75): A client's screens built as a browser-only app and stored as versions; `screens.publish` moves the published pointer and moves it back; a draft used at a preview address on live data; the web serves the published version, by Worker Loader if its spike passes. [Its spec](https://github.com/timoconnellaus/composable-os/blob/main/docs/stages/75-screen-versions.md).
- **The desktop app** (stage 76): One signed app on Electrobun for every client: it downloads a client's published screens, checks them, keeps them on disk and shows them offline; tokens and a fixed bridge in its main process; storage per client; macOS first. [Its spec](https://github.com/timoconnellaus/composable-os/blob/main/docs/stages/76-desktop-app.md).
- **An organisation's own admin screens** (stage 77): A screen as a saved record of which block fills each region; standard blocks updated by a release, declared fills with no code, and custom blocks written as JSX in an extension, run on the host in the organisation's sandbox and drawn by the admin from a tree of our components, a card in a long list included; edit mode beside the assistant; the admin without the server, as a published version the desktop app loads. [Its spec](https://github.com/timoconnellaus/composable-os/blob/main/docs/stages/77-admin-screens.md).
